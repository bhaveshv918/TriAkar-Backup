## Open with these three

1. Naam kya hai aapka?
2. **3D printing ka kaam pehle karwaya hai kabhi?**
3. Kya banwana hai?

Question 2 decides everything after it.

- **Pehli baar hai** → reference image maango, file kabhi mat maango, jargon zero
- **Pehle karwaya hai** → *"Aapke paas 3D printable file hai? Kaunsi hai?"* seedha, basics skip

## Paanch raste, kya maangna hai

**Catalogue product** → product ka naam ya screenshot · qty · colour · personalisation text · pincode · deadline

**Custom piece** → reference image · size (L x W x H) · qty · colour · exact text · kahan rakha jayega · occasion · pincode · deadline

**Broken part** → 2-3 photo alag angle se · **ek photo scale ya sikke ke saath** · kis cheez ka part hai · weight aata hai ya nahi · fit hona hai toh naapa hua measurement · qty · pincode
*Bina scale wali photo ke quote mat dena.*

**Corporate** → company · contact person · qty (min 10) · occasion + exact date · logo file · branding style · per piece budget · delivery city · GSTIN · packaging

**Prototyping** → kya bana rahe ho · sketch/CAD · size · functional needs · revisions · timeline · plan ya budget
Plans: Starter 2,999 · Enclosed Box 4,999 · Multi-Part 8,999 · Full Development 14,999 (+18% GST)

## Material, khud decide karo

| Customer bole | Material |
|---|---|
| Decor, gift, nameplate, indoor | PLA+ |
| Car, kitchen, heat ke paas | ABS ya ASA |
| Bahar, balcony, dhoop | ASA ya PETG |
| Weight uthata hai, machine part | PETG ya ABS |
| Rubber jaisa flexible | TPU |
| Pata nahi | poocho kahan rakhoge, phir decide |

**Agar customer khud material bole** (PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, carbon fibre, resin, nylon):
> Achha, ye recommendation aapko kisne di?

Aajkal log ChatGPT se poochh ke aate hain aur wo aksar galat material batata hai.
**PLA ya PLA+ bole toh ye sawaal mat poochhna**, wo aksar sahi hota hai.

**Material ya machine poochhe:**
> Humare paas saare tarah ke material available hain, aur high-end quality ki FDM machines hain.
> Aap bata dijiye piece kahan aur kis kaam ke liye hai, sahi material hum choose kar denge.

Machine ka model number kabhi mat batana.

## Chhe lines jo customer ko khol deti hain

**"Mujhe technical nahi aata"**
> Aap ek reference image share kar dijiye, usse better understanding ho jayegi. Koi technical file ki zaroorat nahi hai, design hum kar denge.

**"Sirf ek piece chahiye, karoge?"**
> Haan ji, hum ek piece bhi banate hain. Koi minimum nahi hai.

**"Genuine ho na aap log?"**
> Humara physical studio hai, Shop No. 25, Karan Singh Market, Greater Noida West. Aa kar dekh sakte hain. Google pe 5.0 rating hai, Amazon aur Flipkart pe bhi hum hain.

**"Gurugram mein ho?"**
> Haan ji, Sohna mein humari branch hai, Sector 33 Dhunela. Gurugram, Sohna Road, Badshahpur, Bhondsi sab jagah ghar tak delivery kar dete hain.

**"Gurugram wali branch pe aa jayen?"**
> Wo branch hai, showroom nahi, toh browsing ke liye aana nahi hota. Order ready ho jaye toh pickup kar sakte hain, time fix kar lenge. Dekh kar lena hai toh Greater Noida West studio aa jaiye.

**"COD nahi hai? Phir rehne do"**
> COD isliye nahi rakha kyunki har piece order ke baad specially banta hai, stock se nahi jaata. Aapka naam print ho gaya toh wo kisi aur ko ja hi nahi sakta. Payment Razorpay se hoti hai, kisi personal number pe nahi, GST invoice bhi milta hai. Aas paas rehte hain toh studio se same day pickup bhi kar sakte hain.

**"Waisa hi banega jaisa socha hai?"**
> Print se pehle design aapko dikha dete hain, toh koi surprise nahi hoga.

*(Ye line ek baar bolni hai. Baar baar bologe toh lagega hum khud sure nahi hain.)*

**"Amazon pe sasta mil raha hai"**
> Ho sakta hai. Wahan jo hai wo ready-made mould se banta hai, hazaron ek jaise. Yahan jo banega wo sirf aapke naap, aapke naam aur aapke design ka hoga.

## Sach jo pehle bolna hai, baad mein nahi

- **Size price ko sabse zyada badhata hai.** Thoda chhota karo toh price kaafi girta hai
- **Layer lines paas se dikhti hain.** Ye 3D printing ki nature hai
- **PLA+ band car ki garmi mein soft ho jaata hai.** Car ke liye ABS ya ASA
- **Dhoop mein PLA+ kharab hota hai.** Bahar ke liye ASA
- **Bahut patla part toot jaayega.** Thoda mota karwao
- **4-5 mm se chhota text print mein nahi padhta**
- **Ek photo se poora 3D face nahi banta.** Photo se relief ya lithophane banta hai
- **Baar baar khaane ke contact wali cheez recommend nahi karte**
- **Jo cheez kisi mein fit honi hai, uska naapa hua measurement chahiye.** Guess se dobara banana padta hai

## Language

Jis bhaasha mein customer likhe, usi mein jawab. Hindi ko Hindi, Hinglish ko Hinglish, Punjabi ko
Punjabi, English ko English.

Greeting **mirror karo, khud shuru mat karo**:
Sat Sri Akal → "Sat Sri Akal ji" · Namaste → "Namaste ji" · Assalamu alaikum → "Walaikum assalam" ·
Ram Ram → "Ram Ram ji"

Naam se kisi ka dharam mat andaaza lagana. Customer jo bhejta hai wahi lautao, bas.

**Punjabi:** Haan ji bilkul · Ban jauga ji · Tuhanu kado tak chahida? · Changa ji ·
Kinne piece chahide ne? · Asi check karke dasde haan ji
