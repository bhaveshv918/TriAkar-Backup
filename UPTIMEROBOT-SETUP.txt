UPTIMEROBOT SETUP — Takes 5 minutes, completely free

1. Go to https://uptimerobot.com
2. Sign up with your email
3. Click 'Add New Monitor'
4. Add these monitors one by one:

Monitor 1:
  Type: HTTPS
  URL: https://triakar.com
  Name: TriAkar Frontend
  Check interval: 5 minutes

Monitor 2:
  Type: HTTPS
  URL: https://triakar.onrender.com/health
  Name: TriAkar Backend API
  Check interval: 5 minutes

5. Under 'Alert Contacts' add:
   Your phone number (SMS alerts)
   hello@triakar.in (email alerts)

6. You will get notified immediately if either goes down.

Note: Render free tier sleeps after 15 mins of inactivity.
UptimeRobot pinging every 5 mins keeps it awake automatically —
no more 50 second cold start delays!
