Design Credits & Attribution

Good design deserves credit. This page explains how we recognise the designers behind our products — and how creators can reach us.

Celebrating the maker community

TriAkar exists because of a worldwide community of designers and makers who share their craft. Many of the products we manufacture are based on, inspired by, or adapted from designs created within that community, alongside our own original work. Wherever a product draws on a community design, we name the designer on the product page so the credit travels with the piece — not just a product, but the person behind it.

How we credit designers

  • Every product that originates from a community design shows a “Designed by …” credit with the creator's name or handle.
  • We aim to keep names accurate and spelled as the designer uses them. If we've got something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it quickly.
  • All credited creators retain full rights to their original works. Crediting a designer here does not transfer or claim ownership of their design.

Respecting licences & rights

We make a genuine effort to respect the licences and wishes of the designers whose work inspires us. Design is an evolving, shared craft, and mistakes can happen across a large catalogue. If you are a designer and believe a product credits you incorrectly, uses your work in a way you did not intend, or should be changed or removed, we want to hear from you and we will act promptly and in good faith.

For designers — request a change or removal

If you are a creator and would like a listing credited differently, updated, or taken down, please contact us with the product name and a link to your original design. We treat every such request seriously and respond quickly.

  • Phone / WhatsApp: +91 9217-555-833
  • Or use our contact form — mention “Design credit” in your message.

We'd rather get it right than get it fast — but we'll try to do both.

A note of thanks

To every designer whose creativity makes our shelves more interesting: thank you. We hope crediting you here, and pointing our customers to the people behind the products, is a small way of giving back to a community that gives so much.
