How It Works

From Idea to Doorstep

We make on demand. Nothing sits on a shelf — your order is printed fresh, finished by hand, and sent to you. Here is the whole journey in five steps.

1. Choose or describe

Found something you like? Add it to your cart from the shop. Need something that does not exist yet — a custom piece, a gift, or a broken plastic part recreated? Describe it on the custom order form or send a photo on WhatsApp. There is no minimum order for custom work; single pieces are welcome.

2. Confirm and pay

Standard products go straight to secure online checkout — UPI, cards or net banking through Razorpay, all in Indian Rupees. For custom and replacement work, we review your request and send a quote within 24 hours, along with a preview or confirmation of specifications. Production only begins once you approve and clear the advance payment.

3. We print it fresh

This is what makes us different — every item is printed on demand after you order. We select the right material for the job, whether that is PLA+ for decor and gifts, ABS for heat resistance, or PETG for durable flexible parts. Curious about the differences? Read our materials guide.

4. Finish and quality check

Once printed, each piece is cleaned up, finished and inspected at our Greater Noida West workshop. We check detail, surface and fit before anything is packed — because design quality is the whole point.

5. Delivered or picked up

We ship across India in 3–5 days after printing (5–8 days total), with free shipping on orders above ₹999. Orders below ₹999 attract a ₹99 shipping fee. Delhi NCR customers can collect from our shop at Shop No. 25, Karan Singh Market, Chhoti Milak, Greater Noida West. Follow your order anytime on the Track Order page using your order ID.
