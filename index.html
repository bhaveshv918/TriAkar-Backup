FAQ

Things people ask before their first order

What exactly is 3D printing and why should I care?
3D printing builds objects layer by layer from digital files. For you, it means we can make almost anything, including things no factory bothers to produce anymore. Spare parts, custom gifts, one-of-a-kind items. If it can be designed, it can be printed.
Is the quality actually good? It won't look cheap?
Honest answer: it depends on the design and material. We use PLA+, a premium plant-based material that produces smooth, strong, professional-looking results. Every item is quality-checked before packing. If it doesn't look right, we reprint it.
My appliance part broke and the company doesn't sell spares. Can you make it?
This is literally what we do most. Send us a photo or the broken part on WhatsApp. We'll tell you within a few hours if we can make it and what it will cost. Most replacement parts start at ₹299. See our replacement parts service →
How long does it actually take?
Standard products: printed in 2 to 3 days, shipped in 3 to 5 business days after that. Custom orders: we give you a quote within 24 hours, then production begins once you approve. Delhi NCR customers can also pick up from our Greater Noida West shop.
What if I don't have a design file?
Most customers don't. A photo, a sketch, or even just a description is enough to start. We handle the design. You just tell us what you need.
I want 100 pieces for my company event. Is that possible?
Yes. Bulk and corporate orders are a big part of what we do. Branded items, custom packaging, event giveaways. Contact us for bulk pricing, it gets cheaper the more you order. See corporate gifting → or personalised gifts →
What payment options do you have?
UPI, credit/debit cards and net banking via Razorpay, fully secured. You can also order via WhatsApp and we will confirm manually. Ideal for bulk or custom orders.
What if something arrives broken or wrong?
Tell us within 7 days with a photo. We reprint and reship at no cost. No questions, no arguments.
