FAQ

Questions about replacement parts in Noida

Can you recreate any plastic part?
Most plastic parts, yes. We need a photo (ideally multiple angles) and approximate dimensions. We can work from broken pieces, similar parts, or just the empty space where the part should go.
What if the colour doesn't match exactly?
We stock standard colours, black, white, grey, beige. We can get close but an exact match is not always possible. Most customers find the colour close enough, and function is 100% guaranteed.
Is PLA+ strong enough for appliance parts?
Yes, PLA+ is strong and rigid, suitable for knobs, handles, brackets and most household parts. For high-temperature applications (near stove burners, engines), we use ABS or PETG which handle higher heat.
What if the part doesn't fit?
We guarantee fit. If the part doesn't fit, we reprint at no charge, no questions asked. We continue adjusting until the part fits correctly.
