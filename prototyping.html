What Every Plan Includes

01 Design done for you

You don't need a CAD file. A photo, sketch, rough dimensions or even a verbal description is enough to start. Our team designs the part around your requirement.

02 PLA+, plant-based by default

All plans print in PLA+, a strong, dimensionally accurate, plant-derived material. ABS or PETG can be requested for heat- or impact-resistant use cases, ask before ordering.

03 Wastage absorbed by us

Calibration prints, failed attempts and material lost to design iteration during development never get billed to you. The fixed plan price is all you pay for the confirmed design.

04 More units, priced by weight

Once your design is finalised, ordering additional pieces doesn't cost the full plan price again, they're billed by the grams of filament used to print them.

05 GST billed separately

Plan prices are the design and print fee. 18% GST applies on top, in line with standard GST on 3D printed plastic articles (HSN 3926) in India, and is shown clearly before you confirm.

06 Nothing prints until you approve

We share the final design plan for your personal confirmation before production starts. Nothing goes into production until you are 100% happy with the plan.

Common Questions

What material is used for prototypes?

PLA+ is the default across all plans, a plant-derived plastic that's strong, dimensionally accurate and reliable for casing work. ABS or PETG can be requested where heat resistance or flexibility matters more, just mention it in your enquiry.

Is GST included in the plan price?

No. The prices shown are the design and print fee. GST is billed separately at 18%, in line with standard GST rates on 3D printed plastic articles (HSN 3926) in India.

What happens to failed prints and design wastage?

Any material or time lost to design iteration, calibration or a failed attempt during development is absorbed by TriAkar, not billed to you. You only pay the fixed plan price plus any extra confirmed units.

Can I order more units of the same prototype?

Yes. Once your design is finalised, additional units are billed by print weight (grams of filament used) rather than the full plan price, since the design work is already done.

What if my product needs a bigger size than the largest plan?

Our print bed supports parts up to roughly 32 x 32 x 32 cm per piece. Larger products are usually split into printed sections and assembled, tell us your requirement and we'll quote accordingly.

Does production start automatically once I pay?

No. We share the final design plan for your personal confirmation first. Nothing goes into production until you are 100% happy with the plan.
