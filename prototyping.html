What Every Plan Includes

01 Design done for you

You don't need a CAD file. A photo, sketch, rough dimensions or even a verbal description is enough to start. Our team designs the part around your requirement.

02 PLA+, plant-based by default

All plans print in PLA+, a strong, dimensionally accurate, plant-derived material. ABS is available on every plan (a small add-on on Starter/Enclosed Box, included free to choose on Multi-Part/Full Development) for heat- or impact-resistant use cases.

03 Wastage absorbed by us

Calibration prints, failed attempts and material lost to design iteration during development never get billed to you. The fixed plan price is all you pay for the confirmed design.

04 More units, priced by weight

Once your design is finalised, ordering additional pieces doesn't cost the full plan price again, they're billed by the grams of filament used to print them.

05 GST billed separately

Plan prices are the design and print fee. 18% GST applies on top, in line with standard GST on 3D printed plastic articles (HSN 3926) in India, and is shown clearly before you confirm.

06 Nothing prints until you approve

We share the final design plan for your personal confirmation before production starts. Nothing goes into production until you are 100% happy with the plan.

07 A wide material range on request

Beyond PLA+ and ABS, we stock PLA-CF, PETG, PETG-CF, PETG-HS, ASA and Basic PETG for advanced strength, heat or fibre-reinforced needs. Mention your use case in the enquiry and we'll recommend the right one, priced separately from the plan.

Common Questions

What material is used for prototypes?

PLA+ is the default across all plans. ABS is available on every plan too, a small add-on on Starter and Enclosed Box, included free to choose on Multi-Part and Full Development. PLA-CF, PETG, PETG-CF, PETG-HS, ASA and Basic PETG can also be requested, priced separately.

Is GST included in the plan price?

No. The prices shown are the design and print fee. GST is billed separately at 18%, in line with standard GST rates on 3D printed plastic articles (HSN 3926) in India.

What happens to failed prints and design wastage?

Any material or time lost to design iteration, calibration or a failed attempt during development is absorbed by TriAkar, not billed to you. You only pay the fixed plan price plus any extra confirmed units.

Can I order more units of the same prototype?

Yes. Once your design is finalised, additional units are billed by print weight (grams of filament used) rather than the full plan price, since the design work is already done.

What does "1 piece" or "1 complete box" actually mean?

A single part plan gives you 1 printed piece. An enclosed box is built from 2 parts, a lid and a back, and "1 complete box" means both parts, ready to snap or screw together. Multi-part plans include every part needed for 1 full assembled unit.

What if my product needs a bigger size than the largest plan?

Our print bed supports parts up to roughly 32 x 32 x 32 cm per piece. Larger products are usually split into printed sections and assembled, tell us your requirement and we'll quote accordingly.

Does production start automatically once I pay?

No. We share the final design plan for your personal confirmation first. Nothing goes into production until you are 100% happy with the plan.

Can I choose ABS instead of PLA+?

Yes, on every plan. On Starter and Enclosed Box, ABS is a ₹699 add-on over the plan price. On Multi-Part and Full Development, ABS is already included, you simply choose it instead of PLA+ at no extra cost.

Do you need to see the electronics I'm fitting inside?

If your prototype has a PCB, buttons, a battery, a display or any other component going inside, tell us the sizes, quantities and where they should sit. It directly shapes the internal layout, mounts and cutouts, so the more detail you share, the better the first-time fit.

How many design revisions do I get?

Starter has no revisions, so review the design carefully before you confirm it. Enclosed Box includes 2 revisions usable within 31 days, Multi-Part includes 4 within 31 days, and Full Development includes 5 within 61 days. Revisions beyond that, or after the window closes, are quoted separately.

Can I cancel or get a refund after ordering?

Once you approve the final design, production begins and the order becomes non-refundable, in line with our standard custom order policy. Before that approval, you can cancel at no cost, since nothing has gone into production yet.
