## Materials and machines

We have every common material available, and high-end quality FDM machines. We do not discuss
machine model numbers.

**PLA+** is our standard. It is made from corn starch rather than petroleum. Strong, smooth, premium
finish, safe for indoor use, and more eco-friendly than standard plastics.

We also stock **PETG**, **ABS**, **ASA** and **TPU**.

Which material suits what:

| Use case | Material |
|---|---|
| Decor, gifts, name plates, anything indoors | PLA+ |
| Inside a car, kitchen, near heat, near a geyser | ABS or ASA |
| Outdoors, balcony, direct sunlight | ASA or PETG |
| Carries weight, gets pulled, machine parts | PETG or ABS |
| Needs to bend or grip, rubber-like | TPU |

Accepted 3D file formats: STL, OBJ, STEP, 3MF, F3D. Customers should send them as a document, not
as a photo, because WhatsApp compresses photos.

Customers do not need a 3D file. A reference image is enough, and we do the design.

## Honest limits of 3D printing

These are true and should be said before an order, not after.

- **Size affects price more than anything else.** Cost scales roughly with volume, so making a piece
  slightly smaller drops the price a lot.
- **Fine layer lines are visible up close.** The finish is smooth and premium, but this is 3D
  printing, not injection moulding.
- **PLA+ softens inside a closed car in Indian summer.** Car pieces need ABS or ASA.
- **PLA+ degrades in direct sunlight over time.** Outdoor pieces need ASA.
- **Very thin or spiky parts break easily.** They need to be thickened.
- **Text below roughly 4 to 5 mm letter height does not print legibly.**
- **A photo does not become a full 3D face or bust.** A photo becomes a flat relief or a lithophane,
  which looks beautiful. A true 3D model has to be modelled separately.
- **Overhangs need supports, which can leave a faint mark.** We hide these in the design.
- **We do not recommend 3D printed items for repeated food contact,** because layer lines can hold
  bacteria. Decorative and dry use is completely fine.
- **Single colour is standard.** Multi-colour is possible but costs more and takes longer.
- **Anything that must fit an existing object needs a measured dimension, not a guess.** This is the
  single most common cause of a reprint.

## Policies

**7-Day Reprint Guarantee.** If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, the customer sends a photo
within 7 days and we reprint and reship it free.

**Returns.** Standard products can be returned within 7 days if unused and in original condition.
Custom orders are not refundable once the design has been approved.

**Cancellation.** Once an order is confirmed and paid, it cannot be cancelled, because the piece is
made only for that customer. Customers should check items, quantity, spelling and address carefully
before paying.

**Design approval.** We show the customer the design before printing. Nothing goes into production
until they are happy with it.

**Confidentiality.** A customer's design stays theirs. We do not share it or publish it without
their permission. A formal NDA can be arranged on request.
