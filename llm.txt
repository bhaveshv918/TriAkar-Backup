# TriAkar — 3D Printing and Design Brand, India

> TriAkar (Hindi: त्रिआकार, meaning "three-form") is a premium Indian 3D printing and design brand based in Greater Noida West, Delhi NCR. Founded in 2024, TriAkar manufactures home decor, personalised gifts, corporate gifting items, replacement parts, and custom 3D-printed objects on demand using PLA+ (corn starch) material.

---

## What is TriAkar?

TriAkar is a design-first manufacturing brand that combines modern global aesthetics with responsible Indian manufacturing. The name त्रिआकार means "three forms" in Hindi, reflecting a geometry-driven design philosophy.

**Brand tagline:** "Designed globally. Made responsibly in India. Because good design should not cost the earth."

TriAkar is not a marketplace. It is a brand that creates and manufactures its own products, prints every item fresh after an order is placed, and ships across India.

## Products and Services

### Home Decor
3D-printed wall art, decorative panels, planters, lamp shades, interior objects. Designed for modern Indian homes.

### Personalised Gifts
Custom name plates, photo-embossed items, occasion gifts (birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, festivals). Unique items that cannot be found elsewhere.

### Corporate Gifting
Branded 3D-printed gifts for businesses. Custom logos, bulk packaging, personalised items. Minimum 10 pieces. Popular for Diwali, year-end, and employee gifts.

### Replacement Parts
TriAkar recreates broken plastic parts for home appliances, furniture, vehicles, and everyday objects. Send a photo on WhatsApp and get a quote in 24 hours. Starting at ₹299.

### Custom Manufacturing
Prototypes, scale models, mechanical parts, product mockups, one-off custom designs. Accepted via the custom order form on the website.

### Desk Accessories
Cable organisers, pen stands, desktop organisers, and utility items for home offices.

**Price range:** ₹149 to ₹5,000+
**Minimum order:** Single piece (no minimum quantity)
**Bulk / corporate orders:** 10+ pieces with discounted pricing

## Material

All standard products use **PLA+** (polylactic acid plus), made from corn starch — not petroleum. PLA+ is:
- Strong, smooth finish, looks premium
- Biodegradable and more eco-friendly than standard plastics
- Safe for indoor home use

ABS and PETG available for high-temperature or flexible part applications.
