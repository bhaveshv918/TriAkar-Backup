Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, materials, delivery and custom work at TriAkar. Still stuck? Contact us.

What is TriAkar?
TriAkar (त्रिआकार) is a premium Indian 3D printing and design brand based in Greater Noida West, Delhi NCR. Founded in 2024, we design and manufacture home decor, personalised gifts, corporate gifting items, replacement parts and custom objects on demand. Every item is printed fresh after an order is placed using PLA+ material, and we ship across India.
How long does an order take?
Standard products are dispatched in 3–5 business days after printing. Custom orders get a quote within 24 hours, then production begins. Most custom parts are ready in 48 to 72 hours. Delhi NCR orders may arrive in 1 to 3 days.
Do you deliver across India?
Yes. We deliver across India in 5–8 business days, with free shipping on orders above ₹999. Delhi NCR customers can also pick up from our Greater Noida West studio at Shop No. 25, Karan Singh Market, Chhoti Milak.
Can you replace broken plastic parts?
Yes. Send a photo of the broken part on WhatsApp at +91 9217-555-833. We recreate the part and deliver in 5–8 business days, starting at ₹299. We serve customers across Noida, Greater Noida and all of Delhi NCR.
What materials do you use?
We primarily use PLA+, which is made from corn starch rather than petroleum, it is strong, smooth and looks premium. We also offer ABS for high-temperature applications and PETG for flexible, impact-resistant parts. Read more on our materials page.
Can I order a custom design?
Yes. Submit your requirement through the custom order form or WhatsApp +91 9217-555-833. We accept single-piece custom orders, send a quote within 24 hours, and share a preview before production begins. See how it works.
Do you do corporate gifting?
Yes. We offer branded 3D-printed corporate gifts starting from 10 pieces, with custom logos, bulk packaging and personalised items. Popular for Diwali, employee recognition and client gifting across India. See our corporate gifting page.
What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no minimum for standard products, and we accept single-piece custom orders. Corporate and bulk orders start from 10 pieces with better pricing.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards and net banking through Razorpay, our secure online payment partner. All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹).
What is your return and refund policy?
Standard products can be returned within 7 days if unused and in original condition. Items that arrive damaged or incorrect are reprinted free of charge when reported with a photo within 7 days of delivery. Custom orders are non-refundable once the design is approved. See our Refund Policy for full details.
Can I cancel my order after placing it?
No. Because every item is made to order, an order cannot be cancelled once it is confirmed and payment is accepted, this applies in all circumstances, including a change of mind. Please double-check your items, quantity, specifications and shipping address before paying. See our Refund Policy for full details.
Where is TriAkar located?
Shop No. 25, Karan Singh Market, Chhoti Milak, Greater Noida West, Uttar Pradesh 201307, near Cherry County and Ek Murti Chowk. Walk-ins welcome, open Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 8 PM. Call +91 9217-555-833.
How do I track my order?
Use the Track Order page and enter the order ID from your confirmation (format TRK-YYYYMMDD-XXXX). You can also view all your orders in the account section after signing in.
