Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

TriAkar is committed to quality. This policy explains what happens when something goes wrong and what our obligations are to you.

Order Cancellations

Once an order is confirmed and payment is accepted, it cannot be cancelled under any circumstances. At confirmation your order enters our made-to-order production queue, and customer-requested cancellations, including for change of mind, a delay in dispatch, or duplicate orders placed in error, cannot be accepted.

Because every TriAkar item is printed to order and cannot be resold, we are unable to reverse an order after confirmation. Please review your items, specifications, quantity, and shipping address carefully before completing payment.

The only exception is where TriAkar is unable to fulfil an order (for example, a stock or pricing error). In that rare case we may cancel the order ourselves and issue a full refund, as described in our Terms & Conditions. Items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect are handled separately under the sections below.

Standard Products: 7-Day Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of the delivery date for standard (non-custom) products. Items must be unused, in their original condition, and undamaged. To initiate a return, email hello@triakar.com with your order ID and reason for return. You can also reach us at +91 9217-555-833 or WhatsApp.

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the item was defective or incorrect. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days of receiving the returned item.

7-Day Free Reprint Guarantee*

We offer a free reprint within 7 days of delivery if the item arrives damaged or defective, subject to the conditions below.*

* Important, Unboxing Video is Mandatory: To be eligible for a free reprint or return, you must provide a clear, uninterrupted video recorded the very first time you open the package. This is not optional, claims cannot be processed without it. The video must clearly show:

  • The TriAkar-sealed package before opening, our tamper-evident seal must be fully visible and intact.
  • The complete unboxing in a single uncut recording from sealed to fully opened.
  • The condition of the item as received.

Important: Only accept delivery of your package when you can clearly see the TriAkar seal on it. If the seal is missing or tampered with at the time of delivery, do not accept the package, refuse it and contact us immediately. Accepting a package without an intact TriAkar seal will make you ineligible for any claim.

Claims without a valid unboxing video showing the intact TriAkar seal at the start of the recording will not be processed under any circumstances.

Checking Your Package at Delivery

Please inspect our packaging seal before accepting your courier. Every TriAkar package ships with our branded tamper-evident seal. If the seal is broken, missing, or appears tampered with when the courier arrives, do not accept the package, refuse delivery and contact us immediately at WhatsApp or +91 9217-555-833. Accepting a package with a broken seal may affect your eligibility for a return or reprint claim.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, share your unboxing video (see above) along with clear photographs within 7 days of delivery. We will reprint and reship at no charge to you. Claims made after 7 days of delivery, or without a valid unboxing video showing the TriAkar seal, may not be eligible for a free reprint.

We do not offer cash refunds for damaged items, only a free replacement. This is because every item is made to order and cannot be resold.

Custom Orders: Non-Refundable Once Approved

Custom orders are made specifically to your requirements. Once you approve the design or specifications, production begins and the order becomes non-refundable. You will be shown a digital preview or confirmation of specifications before we begin printing.

Before you approve the design or specifications, you may cancel at no cost. Once you approve them, your order is confirmed and enters production, and it can no longer be cancelled (see Order Cancellations above).

Instant Quote Orders

Triakar Instant Quote orders follow the same non-refundable-once-approved rule as other custom orders above. Payment secures your place in the queue at the automated estimated price, but the order does not enter production until you confirm the final customization details with our team; you may cancel at no cost before that confirmation. Once confirmed, the order is non-refundable.

Uploaded 3D model files are not deliverables and are not refundable or returnable, the same as any other digital file provided by or to us (see Refund Exceptions below). The instant price shown at upload is an automated estimate only, actual price may vary 15-60%, see our Terms and Conditions for details.

Bulk Orders

Bulk orders (10 or more units) are subject to a separate agreement confirmed in writing before production. Partial refunds may apply if fewer than the agreed number of units are delivered due to our error.

Refund Exceptions

  • Items damaged through misuse, modification, or use outside intended purpose are not eligible for return or refund.
  • Digital files (CAD files, design files) provided by us are non-refundable once delivered.
  • Orders where an incorrect address was provided by the customer are not eligible for a free reship.
  • International orders are subject to the same policy; however, we are not responsible for delays or damage caused by customs.

Refund Method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via Razorpay. Processing time is 5 to 7 business days after approval. Bank processing times may add additional delays outside our control.

Business Days & Holidays

All timelines mentioned in this policy, processing, dispatch, returns, and refunds, are counted in business days. Business days are Monday to Saturday. Sundays and national or public holidays are not counted, and order processing, dispatch, and support may pause on these days.

Contact

For any return or refund request, email us at hello@triakar.com with your order ID. You can also reach us at +91 9217-555-833 or on WhatsApp. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
