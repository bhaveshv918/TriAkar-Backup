GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE SETUP

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click 'Add Property'
3. Select 'Domain' type
4. Enter: triakar.com
5. Google shows a TXT record to add in DNS
6. Go to Cloudflare/Vercel DNS -> add the TXT record
7. Click Verify in Search Console

After verified:
8. Click 'Sitemaps' in left sidebar
9. Enter: sitemap.xml
10. Click Submit

Google will now:
- Index all your pages within 1-2 weeks
- Show you which searches bring visitors
- Alert you to any crawl errors
- Track your ranking positions

Check Search Console weekly to see:
- Which keywords people use to find you
- Which pages get most clicks
- Any technical issues to fix
